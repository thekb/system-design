I want you to build a service that takes a Twitter hashtag as an input and produces a graph showing how the usage of that tag changed over time. We may want to sell this service to ad providers/agencies so that they could gauge the effectiveness on their ad campaigns on social media, but there may be other use cases where the time horizon is much shorter (hours for a market crash type of event) or much longer (years for a political movement hashtag).
You do not have access to Twitter infra, only to the raw Twitter feed which allows you to read each tweet roughly once (modulo some buffer for retries). You need to design everything from the ground up.
